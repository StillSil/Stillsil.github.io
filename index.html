How to Play Goddess of Logic

1. Layout

In this game, you must place the nikke's in the correct positions on the grid based on the given clues. On the left panel, you will be able to find you current and best time, the current selected Nikke's information, The element cycle chart and weapon ranges. The right side lets you choose accessible Nikke's, as well as filtering and searching for specific information. At the bottom, you will be able to find the Trait markers, which can be placed on individual cells to take notes.

2. Reading Clues

Click on any cell to reveal its clue at the bottom of the center screen. Use details about elements, squadmates, weapon ranges, and neighbor counts to narrow down candidates.

3. Placing Trait Markers

Click an icon from the bottom dock (Element, Weapon, Manufacturer, Class, Burst) and click empty cells to drop custom notes. You can place multiple category markers on the same tile to help figure out which nikke can you on that cell!

4. Submitting Your Solution

Select a Nikke from the right panel and place them on the grid. Once every tile is filled with a Nikke, click VERIFY to check your answer! When pressing the verify button at the top of the screen, you will either be told if you have fulfilled the clues, or told what errors have been made.
