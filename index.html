🎮 How to Play Goddess of Logic

1. Layout

In this game, you must place the Nikkes in the correct positions on the grid based on the given clues. On the left panel, you will be able to find your current and best time, the current selected Nikke's information, the element cycle chart, and weapon ranges. The right side lets you choose accessible Nikkes, as well as filtering and searching for specific information. At the bottom, you will be able to find the Trait markers, which can be placed on individual cells to take notes.

2. Reading Clues

Click on any cell to reveal its clue at the bottom of the center screen. Use details about elements, squadmates, weapon ranges, and neighbor counts to narrow down candidates.

3. Corruption System

Be careful! Some cells will become CORRUPTED on higher difficulties. Corrupted Nikkes will lie, scramble their clue text, glitch their names, or flash strange colors. You must still place the correct character in a corrupted cell to solve the puzzle, but do NOT trust their clues!

4. Placing Trait Markers

Click an icon from the bottom dock (Element, Weapon, Manufacturer, Class, Burst) and click empty cells to drop custom notes. You can place multiple category markers on the same tile to help figure out which Nikke fits that cell!

5. Submitting Your Solution

Select a Nikke from the right panel and place them on the grid. Once every tile is filled with a Nikke, click VERIFY to check your answer!
